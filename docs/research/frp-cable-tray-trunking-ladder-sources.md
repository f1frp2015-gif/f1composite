The manufacturer sample is based on accessible product documentation, not measured market share. Seven system brands/suppliers plus two structural-material manufacturers are represented. General material data must not be treated as cable-system ratings. The article does not claim that F1 has competitors' certifications, supplied their projects, or commissioned an independent engineering review.
