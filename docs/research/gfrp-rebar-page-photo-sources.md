# GFRP rebar product page: third-party photograph sources

Photographs added to `/products/frp-rebar` on 2026-10-27 to show the performance case (corrosion of steel), the ten application segments and placing on site. All were found on Wikimedia Commons through the MediaWiki API, and the license, author and description were read from each file's `extmetadata` on 2026-10-27. Each file was cropped to the page's 8:5 plate, resized to at most 1280 px wide and re-encoded as WebP (quality 76) with metadata stripped; nothing else was changed. Data and captions live in `rebarScenePhotos` in `content/data/frpRebar.ts`; the page lists every credit in its "Photo credits" block.

Rules applied:

- Captions state what the picture shows. The construction scenes are steel-reinforced; none is an F1 or FengDu project, and no caption implies one.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA works are credited with title, author and a link to the license, as the licenses require; the cropped WebP files are derivatives and are published under the same licenses. Public-domain and CC0 works are credited as a courtesy.
- No file labelled "Illustrative photo" (the site's term for stock images licensed from agencies); these are open-licensed and the terms page's image-use clause does not apply to them.
