# Carbon fiber tube and rod pages: third-party photograph sources and property basis

All were found on Wikimedia Commons (category pages `Carbon fibres` and `Carbon fiber reinforced plastic`, plus full-text search through the MediaWiki API); license, author and description were read from each file's `extmetadata` on 2026-10-28. Each file was cropped to the pages' 8:5 plate, resized to at most 1280 px wide and re-encoded as WebP (quality 76) with metadata stripped; nothing else was changed. Data and captions live in `carbonScenePhotos` in `content/data/carbonFiberScenes.ts`; each page lists the credits of the photos it shows in its "Photo credits" block.

Rules applied:

- Captions state what the picture shows. None is an F1 part or project; most of the carbon parts shown were made by wrapping, winding or lay-up rather than pultrusion, and the captions say so where it matters.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA works are credited with title, author and a link to the license, as the licenses require; the cropped WebP files are derivatives and are published under the same licenses. Public-domain works are credited as a courtesy.
- Commercial products that appear (a telescope brand, a drone camera, a robot maker) are shown as application context, not as endorsements.

## Property table basis (`carbonProperties`)

- CFRP: unidirectional pultruded carbon with standard-modulus fiber at about 60% fiber volume. Modulus 120–150 GPa, strength 1,500–2,500 MPa, density about 1.55 g/cm³, rupture strain 1.2–1.8%, longitudinal CTE about −1 to +1 ×10⁻⁶/°C and transverse 20–30: within the ACI 440.1R-15 Table 3.1 ranges for CFRP (modulus 120–580 GPa, strength 600–3,690 MPa, density 1.5–1.6 g/cm³, longitudinal CTE −9 to 0, transverse 74–104 for bars) and the typical values on supplier datasheets for pultruded carbon rod and tube (Easy Composites EC-TDS-Carbon-Fibre-Pultrusions; Rock West Composites product data). Presented as class ranges; the pages say so in the table footnote and the FAQ.
- GFRP: EN 13706-3 grade E23 minimums as published by F1 (`lib/frpDesignBasis.ts`): E 23 GPa, E_T 7 GPa, tensile 240 MPa, density 1.9 g/cm³.
- Aluminium 6061-T6 and structural steel S355: handbook values (E 69 / 200 GPa, density 2.70 / 7.85 g/cm³, CTE 23.6 / 12 ×10⁻⁶/°C, yield 276 / 355 MPa, ultimate 310 / 470–630 MPa).
- Specific stiffness E/ρ: 130/1.55 ≈ 84 (87 on the figure at 135 GPa), 69/2.70 ≈ 26, 200/7.85 ≈ 25, 23/1.9 ≈ 12.

Editorial boundary carried over from 2026-10-25: no F1 CFRP datasheet, certificate, stock size, tolerance, MOQ or lead time is asserted. The numbers on the pages are labelled as published class values and the offered laminate's qualification report is named as the design basis.
