## Publication boundaries

- CFRP is electrically conductive. Glass-fiber tubing needs duty-specific qualification when used for insulation.
- Do not infer torque, pressure, clamp or impact ratings from longitudinal properties.
- Do not inherit fiberglass E23 data, glass-profile calculations or wind-blade laminate reports for this carbon tube family.
- MOQ, tooling, available dimensions, tolerances, sample arrangements and timing remain quotation-specific.
- Application examples are design starting points, not certifications or delivered-project claims.
- The qualitative comparison is not an equal-size load-capacity or cost guarantee.
