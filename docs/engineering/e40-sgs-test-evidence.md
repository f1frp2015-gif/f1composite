## Evidence scope

Both reports were issued by SGS-CSTC Standards Technical Services (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. on 2026-09-07, naming Chongqing Xianju New Material Co. Ltd. Test method: EN 13706-2 Annex D. CM01 individual results: 41.2 / 41.1 / 40.1 GPa, reported average 40.8 GPa. CM02: 41.1 / 42.1 / 41.3 GPa, reported average 41.5 GPa. Three specimens per report; conclusion N/A.

CM01 page 1 specification is 60605 but page 3 lists 2160 × 90 × 90 mm specimens, 1800 mm span, 9 mm/min rate. CM02 page 1 specification is 90905 but page 3 lists 1440 × 60 × 60 mm specimens, 1200 mm span, 6 mm/min rate. Do not silently swap filenames or assign results to a catalog size. Obtain laboratory clarification/corrected reports before size-specific qualification.

Each report limits results to the tested samples and contains an internal-reference use note for research, teaching, quality control and product development. The user explicitly requested public upload of these originals. The website preserves the scope and does not describe them as product certification or structural approval. E40 is a commercial 40 GPa-class designation, not an EN 13706 grade. EN grade reference: https://fiberline.com/european-standard-en-13706 (E17/E23).
