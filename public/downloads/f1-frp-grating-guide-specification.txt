HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE
- Text in [square brackets] is a project value or an option. Replace it or delete the line.
- Loads, deflection limits, fire performance and slip requirements come from the governing
  code, the owner's standard and the engineer of record. This template does not set them.
- Delete the paragraphs that do not apply (for example, molded or pultruded grating).
- Section number: manufacturers commonly use MasterFormat 06 74 13 (Fiberglass Reinforced
  Gratings). US Department of Defense projects use UFGS 06 73 01. Use the number your
  project manual requires.

PART 3 - EXECUTION

3.1 EXAMINATION
  A. Verify that supports are level and coplanar and give at least [1.5 in / 38 mm]
     bearing, or the bearing shown on the shop drawings.

3.2 INSTALLATION
  A. Install panels with bearing bars spanning between supports (pultruded grating).
  B. Fasten each panel with at least [four] hold-downs, at least [two] on each support
     line, at no more than [4 ft / 1,200 mm] spacing, or as shown on the shop drawings.
  C. Field cuts: use carbide or diamond tools with dust extraction; support cut ends;
     seal every cut surface; recheck clip layout after cutting.

3.3 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL
  A. Inspect panel orientation, bearing, clip count and spacing, sealed cut edges,
     gaps and trip edges before handover.
